Built at ROSA

Two interactive dashboards for the FishFORWRD fisheries research inventory · Publishing November 2026

FishFORWRD, ROSA's Fish and Fisheries OffshoRe Wind Research Database, catalogs the research and monitoring projects on fish and fisheries and offshore wind along the U.S. Atlantic coast. I designed and developed two dashboards that turn it into something funders, researchers, offshore wind developers and fishing communities can explore themselves: one shows where research is happening and what it covers, the other shows which research needs are still unanswered.

Both are standalone web pages built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript (the first also uses Plotly) that open in any browser. Instead of reading a static report, visitors filter by location, offshore wind technology, research category, receptor group, project type or gap status, and every chart and count redraws for that selection. Python scripts (pandas, openpyxl, Plotly) read the FishFORWRD Excel workbook, standardize its values and build each dashboard as one self-contained page, so when ROSA updates the database, staff replace the workbook, rerun the scripts and the visuals repopulate on their own.

Dashboard 1 · ROSAPublishing Nov 2026

Research Portfolio Characterization

Shows where offshore wind fisheries science is concentrated and how 273 projects break down. An interactive map sizes each research location by its number of projects and filters by offshore wind technology (fixed, floating or both). Tabs then cover research category, completion status, projects active each year, methodology, receptor group, a methodology × receptor group heatmap that drills into any one receptor group, a research-flow diagram, and the 46 funders by type, where selecting a type lists its funders.

The category chart can play an animation of research growing from 2012 to 2026, filtered by start year and by whether a project is part of a developer's monitoring plan. Categories, methods and receptor groups have hover definitions, and the main charts offer a "View as table" option.

Dashboard 2 · ROSAPublishing Nov 2026

Research Gaps Analysis Dashboard

Checks 70 Summarized Research Needs (SRNs), consolidated from 300+ research needs in 17 prioritization documents by agencies, the fishing industry and regional management bodies, against the research already underway. Each SRN is a card with a gap-status label and six shaded boxes for the regions from the Gulf of Maine to the South Atlantic, darker where more projects cover it.

Opening a card shows a region × receptor group grid that marks every combination with no project as a GAP, with the covering projects on hover. Picking a location recalculates every need's status, and needs can be filtered by gap status (no projects, some coverage, addressed), category, receptor group and project type, searched, or listed in a sortable table.

Aviation Accident Risk Prediction

Combines live weather with 87,944 NTSB accident records (1982–2022). The interactive web page takes a city and aircraft type, fetches current conditions from the Open-Meteo API, explains a weather risk score factor by factor, and compares fatal-accident rates in visual and instrument conditions across aircraft groups. The original Flask app uses OpenWeatherMap and a Random Forest with SMOTE, with Streamlit and Power BI dashboards for accident trends.
