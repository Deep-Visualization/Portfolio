About me

Ocean data matters to a lot of people, and most of them will never open a spreadsheet. I build the views that let them explore it.

I love working with environmental and ocean-related datasets and making them more accessible. At the Responsible Offshore Science Alliance, I cleaned FishFORWRD fisheries research data and designed Research Gaps Analysis dashboards that link research categories, Summarized Research Needs, funding sources and geography.

I care about the whole path: cleaning messy data, choosing the right chart, building filters people understand, and handing over tools that teams can keep updating without me.

Built at ROSA

Two interactive dashboards for the FishFORWRD fisheries research inventory · Publishing November 2026

Both dashboards work like small interactive web pages. Instead of reading a static report, visitors filter by research category, funding source, receptor, status, species or location, and the charts redraw for that selection, so the answer is on screen right away. I cleaned and structured the FishFORWRD data in Power Query Editor so every view draws on the same consistent records, and an automated Excel upload lets ROSA staff refresh and repopulate the visuals on their own.
